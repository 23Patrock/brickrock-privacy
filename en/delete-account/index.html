This page explains how to have your account for the game BrickRock and the player data stored with it deleted — whether or not the app is still installed.

Deleting from inside the app

If BrickRock is installed on your device, this is the quickest route:

  1. Open BrickRock.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Choose “DELETE ACCOUNT & DATA”.
  4. Confirm the safety prompt with “DELETE PERMANENTLY”.

This removes your account and your entries in the leaderboards. The app then creates a fresh, empty profile automatically so that you can keep playing — that new profile contains none of the deleted account’s data.

If your account is linked to Google or Apple, your last sign-in may be too long ago. The app will then tell you that you need to sign in again. Sign in once more and repeat the steps.

Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your account. It only removes the data stored on the device.

Deleting without the app installed

Already uninstalled the app, no longer have access to the device, or deletion in the app is not working? Then request deletion by email:

Request deletion

23patrock@googlemail.com

What to include in your request

To make sure the right account is deleted, we need something that identifies it unambiguously. Any of the following works well:

  • your player name in BrickRock (the name shown in the leaderboards),
  • for a linked account: the email address of the Google or Apple account you signed in with — ideally, write to us from that address,
  • or the user identifier shown in the app.

A purely anonymous account with no link is not associated with any person. Without at least one of the details above, such an account cannot be located — in that case, please tell us your player name.

We confirm receipt and carry out the deletion within 30 days. If there is any doubt that the request really comes from the account holder, we will ask first — that protects you from someone else having your account deleted.

Those processing jobs are subject to a maximum lifetime of 30 days in normal operation anyway. When an account is deleted, we do not wait for that: they are deleted straight away.

Your previous friend code is permanently retired and never issued again. All that remains is a technical block entry recording the time it was retired; the user identifier and display name are removed.

Deletion is final and cannot be undone. Entitlements you have already acquired — the Supporter status in particular — are lost in the process and are not refunded.

Data held by Google and Apple

Your purchase was processed through Google Play or the Apple App Store. The payment and transaction data stored there sits with Google or Apple, not with us — we cannot delete it. Please contact the relevant provider directly:

  • Manage your Google account
  • Apple privacy portal

Your Google or Apple account itself also remains in place. What is deleted is solely your account with BrickRock and the player data stored for it.
